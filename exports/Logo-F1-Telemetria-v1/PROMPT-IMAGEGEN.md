Modalità: strumento integrato `image_gen.imagegen`. Nessuna API o CLI esterna.
Preferenza dell'utente: solo simbolo, nessun nome o testo.

## 01 — Vista dall'alto

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original symbol-only logo for a native Windows Formula racing telemetry application. No wordmark, no letters, no numerals, no text.
Primary request: fuse an unmistakable single-seat OPEN-WHEEL FORMULA RACING CAR and a real motorsport TELEMETRY LINE GRAPH into ONE bold, intelligent, minimal emblem. This is a standalone usable logo, not a presentation board.
Design: geometrically reduced direct overhead silhouette of a formula racecar pointing UPWARD, beautifully symmetrical red aerodynamic chassis with long pointed nose, four short substantial exposed tyre shapes, front and rear wings. A single clean CYAN angular telemetry signal cuts transversely across the lower-middle of the car and extends a little to both sides, showing a rising ramp, a sharp braking drop, then acceleration. The signal is integrated into the silhouette with precise negative-space separation, not randomly pasted onto a detailed car illustration. Car and trace must BOTH read in two seconds.
Visual language: elite motorsport engineering, restrained, fast, exceptionally polished flat vector-style geometry. Compact, purposeful silhouette, precise cuts, balanced negative space, substantial stroke weights, no tiny details. Think an icon that remains recognisable at 32px.
Colors: flat racing red #F04438 for the chassis and tyres, flat bright cyan #64DDF5 for the data signal. Only these two colors and transparent negative space. No white fill and no dark enclosing tile.
Composition: one large isolated emblem centered in a SQUARE image, generous clean 16 percent transparent clear space on every side, no clipping.
Background: truly TRANSPARENT alpha, completely empty around the mark.
Avoid: official F1 emblem, existing racing team marks, letters F or Z, text of any kind, numbers, checkered flags, steering wheels, shields, circles around the emblem, medical ECG heartbeat cliche, dashboard UI, mockups, grids, gradients, textures, lighting effects, glow, shadows, bevels, 3D, photorealism, watermarks.
Prioritize an ownable sophisticated LOGO over an illustration.
```
